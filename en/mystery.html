CASE 001 · UNITED STATES

The D. B. Cooper Hijacking

On November 24, 1971, a person using the name Dan Cooper hijacked a US aircraft and demanded ransom money and parachutes. After the aircraft landed in Seattle and passengers were released, the plane took off again. The hijacker disappeared during the night flight. His identity and fate have never been conclusively established.

Whether he survived the jump or where he landed remains unknown. Unverified theories are not presented as facts.

KOREA · CASE 001

The Daegu Frog Boys Case

On March 26, 1991, five schoolboys went missing after heading to Mount Waryong in Daegu, South Korea. Their remains were found on the mountain in September 2002. The circumstances of their deaths and the person or persons responsible have not been established.

This archive treats the case as a documentary subject only. It does not create a quiz about the victims' case, and it avoids unsupported suspect claims.
